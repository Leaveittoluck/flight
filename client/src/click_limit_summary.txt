  - Guard: if (pending || limitReached) return — prevents call when limit is 0
  - On success: calls onClickUsed() to decrement the counter in GeneratorPage

  CHANGED in CTA section:
  - Both buttons: disabled={!!pending || limitReached}
  - When limitReached: shows amber message "You've reached your limit. Upgrade to continue."
  - When !limitReached: shows red ctaError.message as before (API error fallback)
  - The two messages are mutually exclusive — limit message takes priority

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE FLOW WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Page loads → clicksRemaining = 5
2. User clicks "Search flights" on a card:
   a. handleCtaClick fires, pending = 'flight', buttons disabled
   b. POST /api/click called
   c. Success → window.open(url) → onClickUsed() → clicksRemaining becomes 4
   d. ClicksCounter above cards updates to "4 affiliate clicks remaining"
3. After 5 successful clicks → clicksRemaining = 0:
   a. ClicksCounter shows amber "No clicks remaining this month…"
   b. All CTA buttons on all cards are disabled (opacity-50)
   c. Each card shows amber "You've reached your limit. Upgrade to continue."
   d. No API calls are made (limitReached guard)
